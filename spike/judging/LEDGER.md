# Bake-off ledger

Generated by `python spike/judging/ledger.py` from the blind judges' score files in `spike/judging/`. Don't edit it by hand: `ledger.py --check` fails when it is stale.

A packet is one opener with every arm's thread side by side, lettered at random; judges never saw `key.json`. Means are over packets, and n is how many packets an arm was scored in. Compare arms within one round and one judge family only: the rubric, the mix of arms and the judges changed between rounds, and the last section shows how far the same threads moved when judged again.

Paired rows compare two arms packet by packet (arm minus baseline). W/T/L counts packets where the arm scored higher, the same, or lower; p is a two-sided exact sign test with ties dropped. With 6 to 8 packets nothing below about p = 0.03 is reachable unless an arm wins every packet, so read these as effect sizes with honest noise, not as proof.
