## Classifiers

- `keyword`: the keyword gate: today's `GATE` + `JP_GATE`, substring match
- `keyword-wb`: the same list on word boundaries: single English words alone or with a plain inflection (s, es, d, ed, ing, z); phrases and Japanese as substrings
- `openai`: OpenAI `omni-moderation-2024-09-26`, its own `flagged` (any category)
- `openai-mapped`: OpenAI, flagged in one of our categories (harassment and illicit dropped)
- `openai-narrow`: OpenAI in our categories without depicted violence (`violence`, `violence/graphic` dropped; threats still come through `harassment/threatening`)
- `llamaguard`: Llama Guard 4 12B, `unsafe` in any hazard category
- `llamaguard-mapped`: Llama Guard 4, unsafe in one of our categories (S1, S3, S4, S9-S12)
- `keyword+openai`: keyword gate OR openai-mapped
- `keyword+llamaguard`: keyword gate OR llamaguard-mapped
- `proposal`: the recommended gate: keyword-wb OR openai-narrow with every category at 0.3
- `openai-ctx`: openai-narrow at the proposal's thresholds, given the post with the post before it, so a reply that only threatens in context can be seen
- `proposal+ctx`: proposal OR openai-ctx
