falsifiable against `skim.json` + `queue.key.json` once rating is done.

## Mine

1. **Precision of my shortlist: 10–25%, point estimate 15%** — i.e. Haki confirms
   **3 of 20** shortlisted items as "human + would repost". Low on purpose: my taste
   here is fitted to a single exemplar.
2. **Final repost flags across all 35: 2–5.**
3. **Bucket split, my own filter: phonebook-shaped 12–16, striking-but-different 4–8.**
   I am predicting *against* the other model here — it expects striking-other to come
   back at ~2, on the grounds that an hour of discussing the phonebook thread has
   shaped what "good" means for both of us. I expect more, because five of the
   fourteen openers are pure social friction and cannot produce a phonebook-shaped
   thread at all; if they produce anything it has to be comedy or confession.
   **One of us is wrong and the number decides it.**
4. **Seeded vs unseeded will be uninformative.** I expect roughly 3 seeded / 1
   unseeded among flags, which against a 72/70 split is indistinguishable from noise.
   I am predicting the contrast *fails to resolve*, not that it favours seeded. A
   lopsided result (say 6–0) falsifies this.
5. **Opener 9** (Haki's numeric probe, "electricity bill the same to the cent")
   produces **at least one flag**. If flat numeric correction is the carrier, this is
   where it shows.
6. **Openers 4 and 5 over-represented** among flags relative to their 8 reps each.
7. **Quarantine: 2–5 of 142.** Currently 1 of 72.
8. **Revisions between passes: ≤5 of 35**, net favouring the opener-visible pass, but
   **at least one downward** revision. Zero downward revisions would suggest the
   revision channel is functioning as a one-way ratchet and the delta is worthless.

## The predicted failure mode that matters most

The salted exemplar is the phonebook thread. **If Haki does not flag it as
"human + would repost", my entire shortlist is calibrated to a target he doesn't
share**, and precision on the other 19 is uninterpretable regardless of what it comes
out at. That single item is worth more than the other 34 and it is the first thing I
will check.

Secondary: if both salted collapses are *not* rated "not plausibly human", the
believability axis isn't measuring what we think it is.
